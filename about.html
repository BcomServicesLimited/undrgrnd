About · Gold Coast

Movement that builds brave kids

UNDRGRND is a kids movement studio on the Gold Coast. We teach pole, aerial silks and aerial yoga foundations to children aged 6–17 in a safe, supportive, non-competitive space.

Why UNDRGRND

Built around your child

We believe every child can move. Our classes focus on foundations, confidence and fun — not competition or pressure — so kids build real skills at their own pace.

Foundation Focused

Structured, progressive, age-appropriate movement development.

Strength & Awareness

Coordination, control, mobility and physical confidence.

Confidence & Connection

Self-expression, resilience and positive movement experiences.

Move at Your Own Pace

Individual progression in a safe, inclusive environment.

Safety First

Safe hands for your child

Our instructors are qualified and hold a current Working With Children Check. Classes are kept small, equipment is professionally installed and regularly inspected, and every child is supported to progress safely.
