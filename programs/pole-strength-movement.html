Core Focus Areas

What every Pole Strength & Movement class at UNDRGRND Movement is designed to achieve

Conditioning & Strength Building

Targeted exercises to build the grip, upper body, and core strength required for advanced pole techniques.

Simple Holds & Static Positions

Learn and refine static holds — positions where the body is supported on the pole without spinning.

Controlled Movement Techniques

Develop the control to move slowly and deliberately on the pole, demonstrating strength and body awareness.

Progressive Skill Development

Structured progression through increasingly challenging techniques as your strength and confidence grow.
