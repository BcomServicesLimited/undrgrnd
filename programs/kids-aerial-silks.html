About Kids Aerial Silks

An introduction to aerial arts on suspended silks for children aged 6 to 8. Kids Aerial Silks at UNDRGRND Movement builds strength, coordination, and courage — all in a safe, supervised environment.

At UNDRGRND Movement, Kids Aerial Silks is taught by our qualified instructor in a small-group, supportive environment. Classes are designed to be accessible and welcoming to all participants, regardless of prior experience.

Parent Note: This class is for children aged 6–8 years. Our instructor is qualified to work with children and holds a current Working With Children Check. All equipment is professionally installed and regularly inspected.

Core Focus Areas

What every Kids Aerial Silks class at UNDRGRND Movement is designed to achieve

Aerial Silks Fundamentals

Children learn the foundational skills of aerial silks — how to hold, climb, and wrap safely and confidently.

Strength & Conditioning

Climbing and holding positions on the silks builds genuine upper body, core, and grip strength.

Coordination & Spatial Awareness

Working in three dimensions on the silks develops whole-body coordination and spatial awareness.

Courage & Resilience

The aerial environment challenges children to overcome fear and build genuine courage in a safe, supported setting.

What to Expect in Class

A typical Kids Aerial Silks class at UNDRGRND Movement

Safety Introduction (10 min)

Equipment safety, how to hold the silks, and basic safety protocols. Essential for all new students.

Conditioning Warm-Up (10 min)

Targeted warm-up for grip, shoulders, and core — the physical foundations of aerial silks.

Skills Practice (25 min)

Progressive aerial silks skills — climbs, wraps, and positions taught step by step with instructor guidance.

Cool-Down (10 min)

Stretching and cool-down to support recovery and flexibility.

What Is Kids Aerial Silks?

Kids Aerial Silks at UNDRGRND Movement is an introduction to the circus art of aerial silks — climbing, wrapping, and performing in the air on suspended fabric. At our Gold Coast classes, the program is taught progressively, beginning with foundational holds and climbs and advancing to more complex skills as children develop strength and technique.

Aerial silks builds genuine physical strength — particularly upper body, core, and grip — while developing coordination, spatial awareness, and the courage to challenge oneself in a safe environment. Gold Coast children who train in aerial silks develop physical and mental qualities that benefit them across all areas of life.
