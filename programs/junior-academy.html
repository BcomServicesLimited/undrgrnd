A Joyful Introduction to Movement

Junior Academy is where it all begins. Designed for children aged 6 to 10, this program introduces young movers to the fundamentals of dance, movement and beginner aerial in a fun, supportive and pressure-free environment.

Through games, creative dance and guided exploration, children build coordination, balance, flexibility and body awareness — the foundations that make every future movement skill easier. Just as importantly, they grow in confidence, focus and self-expression.

What's included

  • Creative dance & movement games
  • Beginner floor work & flexibility
  • Introduction to aerial & balance
  • Small, fully supervised classes
  • Qualified, caring instructors

FAQ

Does my child need any experience to join Junior Academy?

Not at all. Junior Academy is designed for complete beginners aged 6–10. Classes are playful and pressure-free, building skills from the ground up.

What should my child wear and bring?

Comfortable, fitted activewear they can move freely in, hair tied back, and a water bottle. Bare feet or grip socks — we'll let you know. No equipment is needed to start.

How much does it cost and how does payment work?

$35 per class, paid upfront for the term. The number of classes depends on the weeks remaining in the current term — the Enrol Now button shows the exact amount.
