Find Us

Movement Beyond Four Walls

UNDRGRND Movement isn't confined to a single space. Our classes run across multiple environments — from our studio to the parks and beaches of the Gold Coast — because movement should be as free as the people who practice it. Class locations are flexible and confirmed when you get in touch.

Gold Coast's Premier Dance School and Yoga Studio

UNDRGRND Movement is Gold Coast's underground movement revolution — a community across Surfers Paradise and the wider Gold Coast dedicated to foundational movement for every body. Whether you are stepping onto a dance floor for the first time, returning to movement after injury, or looking for a creative physical practice beyond the ordinary gym, UNDRGRND Movement offers a home for your journey — with classes in dance, pole fitness, yoga, aerial arts, and recovery movement.

A Dance School Built on Foundation, Not Performance

Most dance schools on the Gold Coast are built around performance and competition. UNDRGRND Movement is different. Our philosophy is rooted in personal transformation — building the foundational movement literacy that changes how you live in your body every day. Our classes welcome complete beginners, people returning from injury, and anyone who has ever felt that movement spaces were not built for them.

Pole, Yoga & Aerial — Safe, Structured, Empowering

Our beginner-friendly Gold Coast classes span pole fitness, Fusion Yoga, and Aerial Silks — building strength, flexibility, coordination, and artistry at your own pace, with patient, expert instruction and a community that celebrates progress over perfection. No prior experience is necessary to begin.

Recovery Movement — Specialised Classes for Rehabilitation

UNDRGRND Movement is one of the few Gold Coast studios to offer a dedicated Recovery Movement Flow program for individuals recovering from injury, surgery, or stress. Using slow, mindful movement with controlled range of motion, it supports a safe return to activity. Medical clearance is required, and we work collaboratively with registered counsellors and healthcare professionals.

Kids Dance & Movement Classes

We offer a comprehensive range of kids' classes including Creative Dance, Urban Mix / Dance Moves, Modern Contemporary, Kids Yoga, Kids Aerial Yoga, Kids Aerial Silks, and Kids Pole Fitness — taught by qualified instructors in a safe, supervised environment for families across Surfers Paradise, Broadbeach, and surrounding suburbs.
