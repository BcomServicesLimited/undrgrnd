FAQ

Does my child need any experience for Junior Academy?

No. Junior Academy is designed for children aged 6–10 of all levels, including complete beginners. Programs build skills from the ground up in a supportive, pressure-free environment.

What should my child wear and bring?

Comfortable, fitted activewear they can move freely in, hair tied back, and a water bottle. Bare feet or grip socks — we’ll let you know. No equipment is needed to start.

How much do Junior Academy programs cost?

Classes are $35 each. Each program shows its own length and total when it opens for enrolment — or express your interest and we’ll help you choose the right one.

Is there a uniform?

Yes. Participants are required to purchase the official UNDRGRND program uniform. The cost is $95.00 (incl. GST).
