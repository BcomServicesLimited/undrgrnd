About Pole Fitness Kids — Foundations

Gymnastics-style pole fitness for children aged 6 to 8. Kids Pole Fitness Foundations at UNDRGRND Movement builds strength, coordination, and confidence — taught with a focus on safety and age-appropriate technique.

At UNDRGRND Movement, Pole Fitness Kids — Foundations is taught by our qualified instructor in a small-group, supportive environment. Classes are designed to be accessible and welcoming to all participants, regardless of prior experience.

Parent Note: This class is for children aged 6–8 years. Our instructor is qualified to work with children and holds a current Working With Children Check. All equipment is professionally installed and regularly inspected.

Core Focus Areas

What to Expect in Class

A typical Pole Fitness Kids — Foundations class at UNDRGRND Movement

Safety & Warm-Up (10 min)

Safety briefing for new students, followed by a dynamic warm-up targeting grip, shoulders, and core.

Conditioning Exercises (15 min)

Age-appropriate strength and conditioning exercises to build the physical foundation for pole work.

Pole Technique Practice (25 min)

Guided pole technique — grips, climbs, spins, and positions taught progressively in a gymnastics framework.

Cool-Down (10 min)

Stretching and cool-down to support recovery and flexibility development.

Kids Pole Fitness Foundations at UNDRGRND Movement is vertical gymnastics. The approach is entirely age-appropriate and has no connection to adult pole dancing styles.
