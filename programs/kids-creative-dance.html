About Creative Dance

Imaginative movement and dance exploration for younger children aged 6 to 8. Creative Dance at UNDRGRND Movement uses storytelling, music, and play to introduce children to the joy of movement.

At UNDRGRND Movement, Creative Dance is taught by our qualified instructor in a small-group, supportive environment. Classes are designed to be accessible and welcoming to all participants, regardless of prior experience.

Parent Note: This class is for children aged 6–8 years. Our instructor is qualified to work with children and holds a current Working With Children Check. All equipment is professionally installed and regularly inspected.

What to Expect in Class

A typical Creative Dance class at UNDRGRND Movement

Welcome Circle (5 min)

Children gather in a circle for a welcoming ritual — a song, a clap, or a simple movement game to begin the session.

Movement Exploration (20 min)

Guided imaginative movement — stories, themes, and games that inspire children to explore how their bodies can move.

Rhythm & Dance (10 min)

Simple rhythm activities and age-appropriate dance sequences that introduce musical concepts through movement.

Cool-Down & Goodbye (5 min)

A gentle closing ritual to transition children back to calm and signal the end of class.

What Is Creative Dance for Kids?

Creative Dance at UNDRGRND Movement is a play-based introduction to movement and dance for young children. Rather than teaching formal dance technique, Creative Dance uses imagination, stories, and music to inspire children to explore how their bodies can move — building a natural, joyful relationship with movement from an early age.

At UNDRGRND Movement, Creative Dance classes are designed to support the whole child. Physically, the program develops gross motor skills, coordination, balance, and spatial awareness. Cognitively, the imaginative and musical elements develop creativity and early musical intelligence. Socially, the group setting develops cooperation, turn-taking, and confidence in a supportive Gold Coast environment.
