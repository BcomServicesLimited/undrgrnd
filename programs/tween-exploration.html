Explore, Discover, Grow

Tween Exploration meets young people aged 11 to 14 right where they are — curious, capable and ready to find what moves them. This program lets tweens explore a range of disciplines, from urban and contemporary dance to aerial silks and movement conditioning.

It's a stage all about discovery and identity. Tweens develop genuine strength, technique and coordination while building the confidence and self-expression that carry far beyond the studio — all in a supportive, non-competitive space.

What's included

  • Multiple dance styles (urban, contemporary)
  • Aerial & flexibility training
  • Strength & conditioning
  • Performance & self-expression
  • Supportive, non-competitive coaching
