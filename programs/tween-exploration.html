Tween Exploration

Discover your strengths — explore disciplines, build real strength and find your voice through movement.

Explore, Discover, Grow

Tween Exploration meets young people aged 11 to 14 right where they are — curious, capable and ready to find what moves them. This program lets tweens explore a range of disciplines, from urban and contemporary dance to aerial silks and movement conditioning.

It's a stage all about discovery and identity. Tweens develop genuine strength, technique and coordination while building the confidence and self-expression that carry far beyond the studio — all in a supportive, non-competitive space.

What's included

  • Multiple dance styles (urban, contemporary)
  • Aerial & flexibility training
  • Strength & conditioning
  • Performance & self-expression
  • Supportive, non-competitive coaching

FAQ

My child is new to movement — is Tween Exploration still suitable?

Yes. While tweens take on more complex work, the program is beginner-friendly and meets each child at their level, whether they're new or continuing from Junior Academy.

What does a typical class cover?

A blend of dance, beginner aerial and conditioning — building strength, coordination and self-expression as one connected practice rather than separate classes.

How much does it cost and how does the term work?

$35 per class, paid upfront for the term. Classes run by Queensland school term; the Enrol Now button shows the current term and total for the weeks remaining.
